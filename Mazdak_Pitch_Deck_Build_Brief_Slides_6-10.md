## Slide 7: Competition

**Headline:** Our real competitor is cheap Fe-EDTA, and our real substitute is acid

**On-slide content (table)**

| Option | Strength | Weakness |
|---|---|---|
| Commodity Fe-EDTA via Maritime dealers | Cheap, stocked, trusted | Expected to lose effect above media pH of about 6.0 to 6.5 [assumption] |
| Sprint 330 (BASF Fe-DTPA) | Known brand, wider pH range on label [evidence: BASF label] | Priced above Fe-EDTA [assumption] |
| Sprint 138 (BASF Fe-EDDHA) | Built for strongly alkaline soils | Expensive for container media [assumption] |
| Acid injection and acidifying fertiliser | Growers already own injectors [assumption] | Constant management; a corrective drench still needed once chlorosis appears |
| Doing nothing, culling | No cost | Lost saleable plants at ship date |

**Our position (one line under the table):** Fe-EDTA chemistry with field evidence of working in alkaline soil, applied to the root zone [evidence: Iranian field trials; root-zone application confirmed by Dr. Kalateh, 23 September 2026]. Whether it works above pH 6.0 in container media is not yet measured [assumption].

**Suggested visual:** a 2 x 2 with "cost per gram of iron" on one axis and "effective pH range" on the other. Place Mazdak with a dotted outline and a question mark on the pH axis, because that position is the claim the UNB test must prove.

**Speaker notes**

- Price per litre compares nothing on its own because iron per litre varies widely. Compare per gram of iron. Mazdak at CAD 25/L and 65.2 g/L iron is about CAD 0.38 per gram of iron [assumption for price; evidence for iron content: SPECTRO assay, 11 May 2023]. Sprint 330 is about CAD 0.51 per gram of iron from a US retail listing at an assumed 1.37 exchange rate [evidence: Data Room 03]. That is a different chelate class and is shown for reference only, not as a cost-advantage claim.
- Fe-EDTA is generally the cheapest class per gram of iron, so the like-for-like comparison is likely to be less favourable to us than the Sprint 330 row suggests [assumption].
- [placeholder] Named Fe-EDTA products, Maritime dealer prices and iron content: quotes from Halifax Seed, Plant Products and Cavendish Agri Services. No cost advantage is stated until those quotes are in hand.
- The pivot, stated before anyone asks: if the UNB test does not confirm extended-pH performance against recorded pH readings, we are a conventional Fe-EDTA competing on price in a segment whose annual iron spend is under CAD 0.3M, and the target market gets rebuilt [Data Room 04].

**Do not put on this slide:** "no competition," the withdrawn 25 percent figure, or the 20-minute process speed as a competitive advantage to the grower. Process speed is a cost story for investors (Slide 9), not a reason a grower switches.
